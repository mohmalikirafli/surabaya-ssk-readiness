01 / THE PROGRAM

Readiness is more than registration.

Sekolah Siaga Kependudukan (SSK) integrates population issues into lessons and student life. A school’s implementation is reflected in formal commitment, curriculum integration, learning resources, student participation, and support systems.

“The largest opportunity is not initial enrolment, but helping willing schools translate commitment into complete implementation.”

05 / METHOD & INTERPRETATION

A field-based implementation assessment.

The team combined direct observation, interviews, document checks, recapitulation, descriptive mapping, and a chi-square analysis. The assessment covered 166 SMP and SMA/SMK-level schools in Surabaya.

Analytical note

Participation in SSK socialisation was associated with possession of a principal’s SSK decree (continuity-corrected χ² = 7.378; p = 0.007; n = 166). This is an association, not evidence of causality.
